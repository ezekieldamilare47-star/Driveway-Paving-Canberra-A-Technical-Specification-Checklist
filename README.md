## Stage 4: Plan Drainage

| Risk | Consequence | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Water reaching bedding layer | Pavers sink and move | Drainage planned at design stage |
| Surface pooling | Slippery surface, accelerated failure | Channel drain or pit box |
| Large impervious area | Runoff toward house or boundary | Permeable paving option |

## Stage 5: Plan for Repairs

| Defect | Intervention Level | Repair Method |
|---|---|---|
| Loose, broken or missing pavers | Any | Replace or re-bed |
| Individual paver level change | Greater than 15 mm | Replace or re-bed |
| Sinking pavers | Significant water pooling | Re-level, replace or re-bed |
| Slippery surface | Oil or moss on surface | Pressure wash, replace or re-bed |
| Water pooling | Any pooling | Channel drain or pit box |
| Tree roots | Roots affecting pavers | Cut roots and replace segment |

### Root Causes to Rule Out First

- Incorrect compaction during base preparation
- No or poor drainage
- Failed edging
- Loads greater than the pavement was designed to carry
- Tree roots
